🏆 Cricket World Cup 2011 Final 🏏

India vs Sri Lanka - Wankhede, Mumbai

On April 2, 2011, India achieved a historic victory against Sri Lanka in the Cricket World Cup final, winning by 6 wickets and securing their second world title after 28 years.

🔥 Key Moment: Dhoni's Six

With 4 runs needed for victory, MS Dhoni sealed the triumph with an impressive six over long-on against Nuwan Kulasekara, unleashing euphoria in the stadium.

🌟 Outstanding Players

🎯 Zaheer Khan - The Destroyer

Zaheer started the match with an amazing streak of three maiden overs, finishing with figures of 2/60. His precision was key to containing Sri Lanka.

🏏 Gautam Gambhir - The Backbone

When India lost early wickets, Gambhir played a crucial innings of 97 runs, stabilizing the team and bringing them closer to victory.

🔥 MS Dhoni - The Invincible Captain

With an impeccable performance, Dhoni scored 91* runs and finished the match with an iconic final six, securing his place in cricket history.
